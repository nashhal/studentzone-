# Yemen Study — Grade 12 Aden learning hub

## Scope and status

This is an original, in-site learning layer for the Aden-first Grade 12 catalogue. It includes **57 topic/unit modules** across the scientific, literary and shared subjects. The content is not a verbatim copy of textbook pages and does not replace the printed school edition.

The hub is a substantial first curriculum release, **not a claim that every individual lesson and every assessment exercise in every textbook has been completed**. The published units are mapped to available 2026 tables of contents; the remaining lesson-by-lesson comparison with the exact school-distributed edition is still a quality-control task.

## Regional handling and provenance

- The book library defaults to Aden. Records labelled Aden and records explicitly labelled shared between Aden and Sana'a are included in the Aden view.
- Hadramout records remain available in a separate regional/source filter and are not silently mixed into Aden's default view.
- The 2026 catalogue used for book names and edition mapping is an independent index, not a Ministry approval notice. The official Ministry e-learning portal should be used to check the approved edition distributed by the school.
- Public textbook contents pages are used to cross-check unit titles and order. If a title is not clearly confirmed in the contents index, it is labelled as an introductory or supporting topic rather than asserted to be an official numbered lesson.

**Important:** “Represented” means there is original explanatory content for the stated unit/topic group. It does not mean that each named textbook lesson, poem, reading passage, workbook activity, or every end-of-unit exercise is individually reproduced or fully assessed. Literature lessons are explained and analysed without copying protected poems or long passages.

## Learning experience in each published module

Every module includes:
1. Learning outcomes and an original Arabic academic introduction.
2. Structured explanation with section headings, key concepts and formulas where relevant.
3. A worked example or applied analysis.
4. A detailed revision guide with definitions, rules/formulas, a method for solving or analysing questions, and common mistakes.
5. A short memory checklist.
6. A quiz divided into **Good / Hard / Hardest**. Difficulty levels apply to quizzes only; lessons and revision notes are kept in the textbook learning sequence.
7. Instant answer checking, explanations and score calculation.

## Current counts

- **57** modules across the listed tracks and subjects.
- **513** original multiple-choice questions: 57 modules × 3 difficulty levels × 3 questions.
- Every module has a dedicated detailed revision guide.
- Each quiz question has an answer key and explanation.
- Quiz attempts are saved in the current browser's local storage under `yemen-study-grade12-learning-attempts`.

## PDF export

The revision sheet and quiz sheet have separate print layouts sized for A4. The student selects **Save as PDF** in the browser's print dialog. The revision PDF includes the lesson summary, definitions, rules/formulas, solution method, common mistakes and memory checklist. The quiz PDF includes the selected difficulty level and a student name/date area.

## Quality and limitations

- Explanations, examples, revision guides and questions are written in original wording.
- Textbook PDFs and protected poems/readings are not copied into the repository.
- Religious studies content is educational and introductory; detailed jurisprudential rulings should be checked against the assigned textbook and qualified teacher.
- Nuclear topics remain conceptual and safety-focused.
- The independent index is used for catalogue mapping, not presented as official Ministry verification.
- The printed Aden edition used by the student's school remains the final reference for lesson sequence, terminology and examinable scope.
