# Yemen Study — Grade 12 Aden learning hub

## Scope and status

This branch contains an original in-site learning layer for the Aden-first Grade 12 catalogue. It includes **65 topic/unit modules** across the scientific, literary and shared subjects. The explanations, examples, summaries and quizzes are newly written; textbook chapters and protected literary works are not copied into the repository.

This is a substantial curriculum release, **not a claim that every paragraph, every exercise, every practical activity, every English reader passage or every end-of-unit assessment in every textbook has been individually reproduced**. The published modules cover the unit headings and selected lesson clusters shown in the publicly available 2026 contents indexes. Exact lesson-by-lesson comparison with the printed school-distributed copy remains the final quality-control reference.

## Regional handling and provenance

- The book library defaults to Aden. Records labelled Aden and records explicitly labelled shared between Aden and Sana'a are included in the Aden view.
- Hadramout records remain available in a separate regional/source filter and are not silently mixed into Aden's default view.
- The 2026 catalogue used for book names and contents mapping is an independent index, not a Ministry approval notice. The official school edition distributed to the student remains the authority for the exact examinable wording and sequence.
- The available contents pages were checked for the major science, mathematics, English, history and contemporary geography titles. The Aden catalogue pages explicitly identify the Aden reading, grammar, literature, Quran, faith, Hadith/Fiqh and Seerah editions.
- Where a subject index gives only broad titles or the available entry does not expose a full contents list, the relevant module is labelled as an introductory or selected-topic guide, not as a page-perfect transcription of the complete book.

### Coverage notes by subject

- **Physics:** the nine textbook unit headings are represented in five modules, from momentum/projectiles through AC, electronics, atomic physics, radiation/matter, nuclear physics, solar energy and cosmology. The modules explain concepts and selected applications; every practical activity is not separately reproduced.
- **Chemistry:** nine unit headings are represented in six modules, covering transition elements, thermochemistry, electrochemistry, nuclear chemistry, organic nitrogen compounds, biochemistry, petroleum, chemical industries and environment.
- **Biology:** eight unit headings are represented in five modules, from the nervous system and hormonal regulation through reproduction, genetics, molecular genetics, biotechnology, environment and Earth history.
- **Scientific Mathematics:** all seven unit/chapter headings are represented across five modules: complex numbers, counting/binomial theorem, probability, conic sections, solid geometry, differentiation and integration.
- **Literary Mathematics:** the three units—counting, statistics, and integration—are covered across two modules.
- **History:** six units are represented in four modules. Unit 1 now covers the four indexed lessons: European Renaissance, geographical discoveries, European colonialism and Arab awakening. Units 2–6 are grouped in the additional modules.
- **Contemporary Geography:** all six indexed units are represented across five modules: political geography and world classification/human development; economic blocs; transport/communications; trade/tourism; global challenges.
- **English:** all six unit headings are represented in four modules. The reader sections are listed as course content, but each individual reader passage is not reproduced.
- **Reading:** the 13 titles in the Aden contents list are grouped into five modules covering comprehension, knowledge, society, values, science and public-health literacy. The original reading passages are not copied.
- **Grammar and literature:** the main indexed grammar topics and literary periods, schools, prescribed-text analysis and criticism concepts are represented. Individual poem and prose passages are not reproduced; the student analyses the school text using original study notes and short references.
- **Quran, Faith, Hadith/Fiqh and Seerah:** original study modules cover selected core topics. Detailed jurisprudential cases and personal rulings are not generated; the school textbook and qualified teacher remain the reference for those.

## Learning experience in each published module

Every module includes:
1. Learning outcomes and an original Arabic academic introduction.
2. Structured explanation with section headings, key concepts and formulas where relevant.
3. A worked example or applied analysis.
4. A dedicated revision guide with definitions, rules/formulas, a method for solving or analysing questions, and common mistakes.
5. A short memory checklist.
6. A quiz divided into **جيد / صعب / الأصعب**. Difficulty levels apply to quizzes only; lessons and revision notes remain in textbook learning order.
7. Answer checking, explanations and score calculation.

## Current counts

- **65** modules across the listed tracks and subjects.
- **585** original multiple-choice questions: 65 modules × 3 quiz levels × 3 questions.
- **65** dedicated detailed revision guides.
- Each question has four answer options, a validated answer key and an explanation.
- Quiz attempts are saved in the current browser's local storage under `yemen-study-grade12-learning-attempts`.
- The learning library can be searched and filtered by scientific, literary or shared track.

## PDF export

The revision sheet and quiz sheet have separate A4 print layouts. The student selects **Save as PDF** in the browser's print dialog. The revision PDF includes the lesson summary, definitions, rules/formulas, solution method, common mistakes and memory checklist. The quiz PDF includes the selected quiz level and a student name/date area. This uses the browser print dialog rather than a server-generated downloadable file.

## Quality and limitations

- Explanations, examples, revision guides and questions are written in original wording.
- Textbook PDFs and protected poems/readings are not copied into the repository.
- Literary analysis uses original explanations and short textual references only.
- Health text on HIV is factual and non-stigmatising; it does not replace medical advice.
- Religious studies content is educational. Detailed jurisprudential rulings should be checked against the assigned textbook and qualified teacher.
- Nuclear topics remain conceptual and safety-focused.
- The independent contents index is used for curriculum mapping, not presented as Ministry approval.
- The printed Aden edition used by the student's school remains the final reference for exact lesson sequence, terminology and examinable scope.
