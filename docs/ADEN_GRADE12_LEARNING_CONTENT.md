# Yemen Study — Grade 12 Aden learning content

## Purpose

This document records the scope and provenance of the current Grade 12 learning hub. It is an original, in-site learning layer: it does not embed copied textbook chapters or replace the student's school edition.

## Regional and edition handling

- The Grade 12 book library defaults to Aden and includes records identified as Aden or shared with Aden by the independent 2026 catalogue.
- Science subject catalogues are sometimes listed as Yemen-wide/common editions rather than as separate Aden editions. Their exact use should be checked against the printed book distributed by the student's school.
- The 2026 catalogue is independent, not a Ministry approval notice. The Ministry's e-learning portal remains the official place to check the currently approved school edition.
- Hadramout records remain available as a separate regional filter and are not silently mixed into the Aden default.

## Learning hub currently published in this branch

Each published topic contains an original explanation, section headings, learning outcomes, key points, a worked example, an expanded revision guide, and a quiz with three difficulty levels. The levels (Good / Hard / Hardest) apply to quizzes only; lessons and summaries remain in the textbook learning sequence.

## Expanded revision notes

Each topic's revision sheet contains:
1. Key terms and definitions.
2. Rules, relationships and formulas.
3. A repeatable method for solving or analysing questions.
4. Common mistakes to avoid.
5. A short final memory checklist.

The printable A4 PDF includes the lesson outline, formulas and bullet points, a worked example, the revision guide and the final checklist. The browser print dialog is used; the student chooses **Save as PDF**.

## Quiz structure and validation

- 14 topic modules are currently represented.
- Three levels per topic: Good, Hard, and Hardest.
- Three multiple-choice questions per level.
- Each answer has an explanation, and the UI supports immediate checking and score submission.
- Expected total in this batch: 14 × 3 × 3 = 126 questions.
- Quiz results are stored locally in the current browser under `yemen-study-grade12-learning-attempts`.

## Important limits

- This is a partial content batch, not a complete Grade 12 curriculum.
- Remaining units and subjects still need to be written and reviewed.
- The independent catalogue helps map titles and sequence; it is not proof of official Ministry approval.
- Lesson explanations and test questions are written afresh. Textbook PDFs are not copied into this repository.
- Before describing a topic as fully exam-ready, compare it with the exact printed Aden edition used by the school and review the final topic sequence with a qualified subject teacher.
