# Grade 12 pilot content batch — Yemen Study

Release candidate: 19 November 2026

## What this batch adds

- An in-site Physics study page at `/grade-12-study`.
- An original Arabic explanation and review summary for scalar and vector quantities.
- One worked example for perpendicular displacements.
- Fifteen original multiple-choice practice questions split across three selectable levels: جيد (5), صعب (5), الأصعب (5).
- Immediate answer checking and short explanations.
- A result record saved locally in the student's current browser.
- A print stylesheet and a PDF workflow through the browser's Print → Save as PDF dialog. The student can optionally include the answer key and explanations.

## Curriculum and edition status

This is a learning-content pilot for the existing Physics demonstration topic. It is **not** a claim that the topic order or title has been verified against the full Yemeni Grade 12 textbook for a particular regional authority or 1448 AH edition.

The Ministry e-learning portal lists digital Yemeni books from Grade 1 through Grade 12, and ministry-channel announcements have published Grade 12 book collections for the 2025 school year. The portal and edition-specific book files still need to be opened and checked page by page before the national Grade 12 curriculum map can be marked complete.

- Ministry e-learning portal: https://e-learning-moe.edu.ye/
- Ministry book catalogue: https://e-learning-moe.edu.ye/book.php
- Ministry Grade 12 book announcement archive (2025): https://t.me/yemeneducation2/42213

## Scientific references used for verification

- OpenStax, University Physics Volume 1, section 2.1, “Scalars and Vectors”: https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/2-1-scalars-and-vectors

The content in Yemen Study is newly written and independently phrased. The external reference is for concept verification, not a student-facing dependency or a source of copied lesson text.

## Not included in this release

- The full Grade 12 Physics textbook or every chapter.
- Verified subject-by-subject lesson mapping for the scientific or literary track.
- Grade 12 literary-track lesson coverage.
- Downloading or re-hosting copyrighted Ministry textbook PDFs.
- Server-side progress sync or database-backed attempts; this pilot uses browser-local storage.
- A binary PDF file hosted by the site. PDF output is generated through the browser's print-to-PDF workflow.
