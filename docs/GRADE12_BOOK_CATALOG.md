# Grade 12 book catalogue — Yemen Study

Checked: 19 November 2026

## Purpose and scope

The Grade 12 book library at `/grade-12-books` lists 25 school-book files across the scientific and literary tracks. It contains a searchable catalogue, filters for shared/scientific/literary books, and a link to the source-held file plus an internal Yemen Study subject page.

## Source provenance

Primary file index used for the 25 source links:

- Hadramout Education Office, Ministry of Education, curriculum catalogue: https://meoh.gov.ye/services/subjects/
- The Grade 12 section lists 25 files (books, activities and exercise books) for the third secondary grade. The catalogue is regional to Hadramout and does not specify a publication year or edition for every file. Do not represent these links as verified 1448 AH files or as applicable to every governorate.

Current national publication pointer:

- Ministry e-learning portal / books catalogue: https://e-learning-moe.edu.ye/book.php
- Ministry books channel announcement for 1448 AH / 2026–2027: https://tg.me/books_yemen_new/1316
- The ministry announcement points to the national portal, but the exact Grade 12 book files and their regional variants have not yet been inspected from that portal. Therefore the 25 catalogue entries are labelled as regional source copies with edition not stated.

Secondary cross-checks (not government sources):

- e-Najah Grade 12 index: https://e-najah.net/CuLrTHS/
- YemBooks Grade 12 2026 index: https://yembooks.com/books/stage-secondary/sec-3
- These pages can help compare titles and track groupings, but they are not treated as authority for national curriculum approval or edition equivalence.

## Rights and delivery

- The app records catalogue metadata and links to the original host. It does not copy or re-host the PDF files in the Yemen Study repository.
- The external source link is for opening the file from the publishing/hosting source. The subject link returns the learner to Yemen Study for the internal study experience.
- A book appearing in the index does not mean that every chapter, lesson, summary, question bank or mock examination has been written and reviewed.
- Current live in-site learning content for Grade 12 remains a limited pilot. The Physics scalar/vector topic is a demonstration and must be mapped against the student's exact regional edition before being called an approved curriculum lesson.
