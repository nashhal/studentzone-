# Grade 12 book catalogue — Yemen Study

Checked: 19 November 2026

## What the live library contains

The route `/grade-12-books` contains **58 catalogue records** and provides track, region, source and text-search filters:

- **33 2026 records** from the independent YemBooks index. These include separate Sana'a and Aden records for titles where the index shows separate versions, as well as entries listed for both regions.
- **25 regional file records** from the Hadramout Education Office catalogue. The portal is a regional government education index, and it does not identify the publication year of every Grade 12 file.

The counts are catalogue records, not 58 unique subject titles. Several records are alternate-region editions or activity/exercise books.

## Source provenance and edition confidence

### Ministry announcement / national portal

- Ministry books channel announcement for 1448 AH / 2026–2027: https://tg.me/books_yemen_new/1316
- Ministry e-learning portal: https://e-learning-moe.edu.ye/index.php
- Ministry book catalogue path: https://e-learning-moe.edu.ye/book.php

The Ministry announcement says the 1448 AH curricula are available through the national portal. During this catalogue build the national book index timed out when opened, so the exact Grade 12 files, file covers, and region-specific editions were **not individually verified** from the portal. The live library therefore does not label the independent index records as Ministry-confirmed 1448 files.

### Independent 2026 cross-reference

- YemBooks Grade 12 index, page 1: https://yembooks.com/explorer/stage-secondary/sec-3
- YemBooks Grade 12 index, page 2: https://yembooks.com/explorer/stage-secondary/sec-3/page/2

The independent index labels 33 Grade 12 records for 2026. It lists separate Sana'a and Aden books for literature, grammar, reading, seerah, Quran, faith, and some Islamic studies subjects. It is useful for tracking the named edition and matching book pages, but it is **not the Ministry's official portal** and is not treated as proof of Ministry approval. The app links to its item pages instead of copying or re-hosting the PDF files.

### Hadramout regional catalogue

- Hadramout Education Office, Ministry of Education: https://meoh.gov.ye/services/subjects/

The Grade 12 section lists 25 book, activity and exercise files. This is an official regional government education index, but not a national edition register. Its item cards are labelled “Hadramout — edition year not specified.”

### Other cross-check

- e-Najah Grade 12 catalogue: https://e-najah.net/CuLrTHS/

This is a non-government index and is used only as a secondary title cross-check.

## 2026 index inventory

The 33 records in the independent index cover:

- **Scientific track:** Biology and activity book, Chemistry and activity book, Physics and experiment/activity book, Scientific Mathematics and exercise book.
- **Literary track:** Literature/Texts (Sana'a and Aden records), Literary Mathematics and exercise book, Philosophy, Logic and Research Methods, Psychology, Principles of Cartography.
- **Shared or broadly listed subjects:** English course and activity book, History, Geography, Grammar (Sana'a and Aden records), Reading (Sana'a and Aden records), Seerah (Sana'a and Aden records), Hadith/Fiqh variants, Faith (Sana'a and Aden records), Quran (Sana'a and Aden records).

The independent catalogue itself sometimes uses “Sana'a and Aden” for an item and sometimes publishes separate regional pages. The app preserves those labels and does not merge separate regional records into a single edition.

## Rights and student experience

- Yemen Study stores catalogue metadata and links to original source/item pages. It does **not** copy or re-host copyrighted textbook PDFs in the repository.
- A book appearing in the library means its source record is indexed. It does not mean that every chapter, lesson, summary, question bank or mock exam has been created.
- Current in-site Grade 12 learning content is still a limited pilot: one original Physics topic (scalars and vectors) with a structured explanation, worked example, revision summary and 15 tiered questions. Its curriculum alignment has not yet been confirmed against an exact regional textbook edition.
- The 2026 and Hadramout records help students locate the right book edition; they are not substitutes for the internal lesson library.

## Release status and remaining work

This release makes the Grade 12 book directory searchable and region-aware. It does **not** complete the Grade 12 teaching syllabus.

To complete the study library without inventing content:
1. Obtain the exact Ministry PDF for each chosen region/edition and check its cover and contents pages.
2. Record the verified unit and lesson order for each subject.
3. Write original Arabic explanations, worked examples, revision notes and questions for every verified lesson.
4. Validate each scientific lesson against authoritative academic references and have curriculum-specific subjects reviewed by a qualified teacher.
5. Keep Sana'a, Aden and regional records separate until source evidence proves edition equivalence.

No book or lesson should be marked complete merely because its source record exists.
