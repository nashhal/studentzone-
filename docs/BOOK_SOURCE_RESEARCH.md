## Editorial workflow for Yemen Study notes

Use a Save My Exams-inspired learning structure without reproducing its text, layout, or protected material:

1. Learning objectives.
2. Short original explanation, organised by key idea.
3. Key terms, formulae, definitions and units.
4. Worked example with reasoning steps.
5. Common mistakes and quick recap.
6. Topic practice at increasing difficulty.
7. Explanatory feedback after submission.
8. Source, exact edition and page references.

Every lesson remains a draft until its source edition is checked and a subject teacher reviews it.

## Rights and hosting policy

- Do not re-host Ministry PDFs or third-party PDFs without explicit permission or a clear reuse licence.
- Link students to the original publisher or file post.
- Yemen Study summaries must be written independently and must not reproduce textbook passages, answer keys or another revision site's notes.
- Record the source, edition, year, rights status and reviewer for every content item.
- If the edition is not confirmed as 1448 / 2026–2027, label it as an archive, not the current syllabus.
