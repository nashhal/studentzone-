# Book source research — Yemen Study

Research date: 13 November 2026

## Primary source

**General Administration of E-Learning — Ministry of Education and Scientific Research**

- Main book portal: https://e-learning-moe.edu.ye/book.php
- Ministry homepage states that PDF textbooks are available from Grade 1 Basic through Grade 3 Secondary, with browsing and direct download.
- This is the preferred source for locating the relevant school-book file.
- Access note: page requests timed out during this research session, so individual file links for all grades could not be enumerated here.

## Regional catalogue

**Education Office — Hadramout Governorate**

- Catalogue: https://meoh.gov.ye/services/subjects/
- Search indexing lists PDF files for Grades 1–12, including separate parts, activities and exercise books for some subjects.
- This is a regional catalogue. Students should confirm that the listed edition matches their school and governorate.

## Editorial workflow for Yemen Study notes

Use a Save My Exams-inspired learning structure without reproducing its text, layouts or protected material:

1. Learning objectives.
2. Short original explanation, organised by key idea.
3. Key terms, formulae, definitions and units.
4. Worked example with reasoning steps.
5. Common mistakes and quick recap.
6. Topic practice at increasing difficulty.
7. Explanatory feedback after submission.
8. Source and edition record with page references.

Every lesson remains a draft until its source edition is checked and a subject teacher reviews it.

## Rights and hosting policy

- Do not re-host Ministry PDFs or third-party PDFs in this repository without explicit permission or a clear reuse licence.
- Link users to the original publishing page or file.
- Yemen Study summaries must be written independently and should not reproduce textbook passages, answer keys or another revision site's notes.
- Every content record should include source, edition, year, rights status and reviewer.
